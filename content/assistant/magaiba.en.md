# MAGAIBA

What MAGAIBA is: $MAGAIBA was the first Argentine memecoin to reach global virality. It was created by Juan Ruocco and Pablo Wasserman, hosts of the podcast Círculo Vicioso, together with two anonymous devs, as a community-driven project.

$MAGAIBA reached a market cap of USD 16,000,000, around USD 350,000 in liquidity and an all-time-high price (ATH) of USD 0.02132.

## Tomás's role in MAGAIBA

MAGAIBA was a side project for Tomás in 2024, mixing web3, communication and artificial intelligence. Tomás was a listener of the podcast Círculo Vicioso and joined the community: he took part in $MAGAIBA's communications and created the YouTube channel RADIO MAGAIBA, which ran 24/7 with AI-generated songs (Suno) and curated community content. The RADIO MAGAIBA channel accumulated over 1.9 million watch hours and more than 500 subscribers.

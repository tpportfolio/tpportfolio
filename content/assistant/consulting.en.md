# Independent consultant

Tomás has worked as an independent Brand, Marketing & AI Consultant since July 2020, for fintech, healthtech, esports and media companies across LATAM. He is a Brand Strategist and Brand Marketing Leader, and combines brand strategy, cultural marketing and creative technology.

His background spans entertainment, aviation, esports and gaming, fintech, healthtech, B2B logistics and audiovisual production.

What he does as an independent consultant:
- designs brand positioning and messaging frameworks for multi-market expansion
- leads integrated go-to-market strategies aligning content, PR and partnerships
- builds influencer ecosystems and brand collaborations to drive awareness and engagement
- develops narrative frameworks and messaging architectures for founders and executive teams
- orchestrates cross-functional execution across Product, Growth, Brand and external agencies
- designs AI-powered creative workflows that compress time-to-market and raise creative output

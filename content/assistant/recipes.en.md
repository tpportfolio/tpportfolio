# Favorite recipes

Tomás enjoys cooking, and these are some of the recipes currently included in the assistant knowledge base:

## Chocotorta
- Core ingredients: chocolate cookies, cream cheese, dulce de leche, cold coffee and cocoa powder.
- Base method: mix cream cheese with dulce de leche, build layers with lightly coffee-soaked cookies and chill for several hours.

## Chipa
- Core ingredients: corn starch, Paraguayan cheese or a mix of hard cheeses, butter, eggs, milk, salt and anise.
- Base method: mix dry ingredients, add butter and cheese, incorporate eggs and milk, shape into rings or small rolls and bake at 200°C.

## Homemade pizza dough
- Core ingredients: flour, dry yeast, sugar, salt, olive oil and warm water.
- Base method: activate yeast, knead, proof, stretch and bake with sauce and mozzarella.

## Butter cookies
- Core ingredients: butter, powdered sugar, egg, vanilla and flour.
- Base method: cream butter and sugar, add egg and vanilla, fold in flour, chill the dough, cut and bake.

## Torta Rogel
- Core ingredients: flour, cold butter, egg yolks, cold water, dulce de leche and Italian meringue.
- Base method: make the dough, divide into thin layers, bake, stack with dulce de leche and cover with meringue.

## Bolognese sauce
- Core ingredients: ground meat, onion, garlic, carrot, celery, red wine, tomatoes, bay leaf and olive oil.
- Base method: build a soffritto, brown the meat, add wine and tomato, and cook slowly for a long time.

## Pomodoro sauce
- Core ingredients: ripe tomatoes or canned peeled tomatoes, garlic, olive oil, salt, pepper and fresh basil.
- Base method: gently brown the garlic, cook the tomatoes until reduced, and add basil at the end.

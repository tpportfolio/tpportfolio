# Profile and positioning

Tomás Peró works at the intersection of brand, content and AI systems.

He has more than 18 years of experience across marketing, advertising and content. He started in agencies, then moved through brands and startups, and today works independently.

His current focus combines storytelling, digital culture and systems thinking for modern teams.

Today he offers consulting, training and support for AI-powered content creation.

He plugs into marketing teams, brands and agencies to accelerate production, iterate faster, reduce friction and improve quality across real image, video, audio and text pipelines.

The portfolio itself also works as practical proof of that approach: a large portion of the site and several visible pieces were vibe-coded or produced with AI support.

He works at the intersection of brand strategy, cultural marketing and creative technology.

The roles he is looking for and identifies with are Head of Brand, Brand Lead, Brand Manager, Brand Marketing Manager, Brand Strategist, Cultural Marketing and Creative Technologist. He also takes consulting projects with brands, agencies and lean teams.

## Career

Tomás trained in agencies: Altheim Comunicaciones, as Account Assistant from June 2007 to June 2008, and J. Walter Thompson (JWT/GLUE), as Account Executive from June 2008 to March 2011.

His brand career began at The Walt Disney Company, where he worked from March 2011 to December 2015 inside one of the world's most disciplined brand systems, in three roles. At The Walt Disney Company he was Promotions Coordinator from March 2011 to May 2014: he led 360° brand promotions and third-party partnerships for Latin America across all of Disney's lines of business, with 100+ promotions per year. At The Walt Disney Company he was Digital Distribution Sales Coordinator from May 2014 to December 2015: he managed digital distribution strategy for the Rest of Latin America with partners such as Netflix, Apple/iTunes and Google Play. At The Walt Disney Company he was also Disney Labs: Corporate Entrepreneur from May 2014 to December 2015, spending 20% of his time on Disney Latin America's first corporate entrepreneurship programme, an early signal of his dual brand and technology profile.

At Air New Zealand he was Marketing Specialist & Market Development from December 2015 to June 2020, and the regional marketing lead for South America (Argentina, Brazil, Chile and Uruguay). At Air New Zealand he was responsible for the brand launch in the region and for the market launch of a new long-haul route, co-developed the 5-year business plan, managed an annual regional marketing budget of over USD 500K and led five agencies (creative, PR, media and activations) under a single brand voice.

Since July 2020 he has worked as an independent Brand, Marketing & AI Consultant for fintech, healthtech, esports and media companies across LATAM. His independent engagements, each with its own dates, are:

- Stone Movistar, Brand & Marketing Consultant, from July 2020 to December 2021: launch and go-to-market of an esports and streaming club.
- Go Broadway!, Marketing Consultant, from August 2020 to November 2020: launch of Backstage in Latin America.
- Airtm, Growth Marketing + R&D, from September 2021 to July 2022: regional acquisition experiments and the Airtalks branded podcast, at a fintech.
- Sinerlogic, Marketing & Branding Consultant, in 2022 and 2023: brand identity, messaging and value proposition for a B2B logistics company.
- DrGea.com, Head of Communications, from February 2023 to April 2024: regional brand narrative and communications for a telemedicine platform.
- $MAGAIBA, Community, Comms & AI Content, in 2024, as a web3 and AI side project.
- Paradise.la, AI & Content Consultant & Producer, from July 2024 to January 2026: he advised the production studio on its transition to AI-enabled creative production.

## Creative technology

Since June 2024 he has been part of Morfeo Academy's course and community. He runs a daily AI stack (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, NotebookLM, Flux, Midjourney, Kling, Veo, ElevenLabs and v0, among others) to prototype campaigns, accelerate content production and expand what a small brand team can deliver. His professional experience directly related to AI is his work as AI & Content Consultant & Producer at Paradise.la, from July 2024 to January 2026, where concept-to-delivery cycles dropped from 2+ weeks to 3–4 days on pilot productions.

# Profile and positioning

Tomás Peró is a strategic brand and marketing leader. His positioning connects three areas: brand and culture strategy; creative technology and AI-enabled content operations; and fractional or interim brand leadership.

He has over 18 years of experience across marketing, advertising and content. He started in agencies, then moved through brands and startups, and today works independently.

His current focus combines storytelling, digital culture and systems thinking for modern teams. His target roles include Head of Brand, Strategy Director and Culture Marketing Manager.

He has explored artificial intelligence as a self-taught practitioner since late 2022. In 2023, he generated a LoRA of himself. Since June 2024, he has been part of Morfeo Academy's course and community. His professional experience directly related to AI is his work as AI & Content Consultant & Producer at Paradise.la, from July 2024 to January 2026.

Today he offers consulting, training and support for AI-powered content creation.

He plugs into marketing teams, brands and agencies to accelerate production, iterate faster, reduce friction and improve quality across real image, video, audio and text pipelines.

The portfolio itself also works as practical proof of that approach: a large portion of the site and several visible pieces were vibe-coded or produced with AI support.

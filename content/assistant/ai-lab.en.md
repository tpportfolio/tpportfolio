# AI experiments and the8bureau

Tomás entered the AI space between 2022 and 2023, first as a self-taught practitioner and later formalizing the path with courses such as Morfeo Academy in 2024.

The focus of AI Experiments is to use tools, systems and workflows that add direct value to brands and companies.

Current stack shown on the site:
- Image: Nanobanana, Seedream, Leonardo, Flux, Ideogram, GoogleFX, Midjourney
- Video: Sora2, Veo3, Higgsfield, Kling, Hailuo, Runway
- Audio: ElevenLabs, fish.audio, Suno, Udio
- LLMs: ChatGPT, Kimi, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude

the8bureau is both the personal AI brand and a conceptual operating system: the goal is to turn it into a private, local and offline toolset.

Highlighted tools in the8bureau block:
- Autonomous Ad Agency
- Neural Optimization Suite (SEO/AEO/GEO)
- Video Subtitle Suite
- Transcribe Anything Now
- Side-by-side Image Compare
- Maps Lead Extractor

In 2026 Works and Experiments, the current preview items are Ad 30-40, Product Demo, Fashion Campaign and UGC Content.
